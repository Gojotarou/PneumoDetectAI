Total Staff

This represents the total number of healthcare staff members registered in the system, including:

  • Doctors: Medical professionals authorized to diagnose and treat patients
  • Nurses: Medical professionals who assist in patient care and data entry
  • Admins: System administrators who manage staff and patient assignments

This count helps you understand the size of your healthcare team and resource allocation.

×
Total Patients

This represents the total number of patients registered in the system across all departments and timeframes.

Note: This includes all patients regardless of whether they have been assigned to specific staff members or have completed X-ray analyses.

This metric helps you understand the total patient base and system utilization. Compare this with "Active Assignments" to identify patients who may need staff assignment.

×
Active Assignments

This represents the number of unique patients who have at least one staff member assigned to them.

Example: If Patient A is assigned to 1 doctor and 1 nurse (2 assignments), they are counted as 1 in this metric.

Key insight: If "Active Assignments" is less than "Total Patients", it means some patients don't have any assigned staff. Consider making assignments for better care coordination.
